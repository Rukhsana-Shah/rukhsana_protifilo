How I Work

Principles Behind Every Ticket

Good support means fixing the problem, explaining it clearly, and leaving a record the next technician can use.

⏱️

SLA-Driven

I triage by impact and urgency, and critical requests get a response within 30 minutes.

📝

Documented

Every resolution is written in a structured Who/What/When/Where/Why/How format.

💬

Client-Friendly

I write in plain language that non-technical users can act on.

🔒

Security-First

Least-privilege access, prompt offboarding and alert triage come before convenience.

WHO → user / requester  ·  WHAT → issue & impact  ·  WHEN → timeline
WHERE → system / tenant  ·  WHY → root cause  ·  HOW → steps taken & outcome
